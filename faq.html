Frequently Asked Questions

What the experience is. What it is not.

Clear answers based on the current Own Your Stage Studio offer and agreement documents.

What is Own Your Stage Studio actually selling?

A done-for-you authority-building experience. The professionally produced virtual panel is the vehicle; the intended outcome is stronger visibility, credibility, connection and recognition.

How long is the Host Experience?

The current source materials are not fully aligned. The Business Concept and Host Agreement describe a three-month engagement, while the current Host Product document says “2 or 3 Month.” This should be finalized before publishing the live sales page.

What is the current launch investment?

$2,997 for the Own Your Stage Experience™.

Does Own Your Stage Studio promote the event?

No. The Studio creates the registration page, branding, promotional graphics and swipe copy. The host and participating panelists are responsible for distributing those materials and generating registrations.

What happens during the live event?

The host agreement describes a professionally moderated virtual event of roughly 60 to 90 minutes. It can include a host opening, moderator introduction, panelist introductions, moderated discussion, individual spotlights, approved complimentary resources, a structured host call to action and limited audience interaction.

Are registrations or sales guaranteed?

No. The current agreements specifically avoid guarantees around registrations, attendance, leads, sales, revenue and other business results.

Who are the ideal hosts?

Established coaches, consultants, founders, authors, speakers, service providers and other subject-matter experts with proven experience who need greater visibility and stronger authority positioning.

How does the Panelist Program fit in?

Panelists are a secondary audience and network-building pathway. They can contribute expertise to a selected event, gain exposure and potentially become future hosts.

What should someone do if they are interested but not ready to buy?

The Readiness Assessment is designed as the entry point. Depending on the result, someone may be directed toward the Panelist Program, a Spotlight Call or the Host Experience.
