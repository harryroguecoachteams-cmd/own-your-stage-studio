Your current level

Four stages of authority visibility.

The labels are diagnostic, not judgmental. The assumption at every level is that you are already good at your work.

01

Hidden Expert

Strong expertise is present, but recognition is concentrated inside a small existing network.

02

Emerging Expert

Your visibility is beginning to expand, but your authority platform is not yet consistent or unmistakable.

03

Expanding Expert

Your reach, positioning and recognition are growing. The next challenge is building a repeatable authority engine.

04

Visible Expert

Your expertise is broadly recognized. Strategic platforms can deepen association, reach and long-term authority.

What the assessment looks at

Recognition is more than follower count.

  • Positioning
  • Recognition
  • Consistency
  • Audience reach
  • Readiness to create an authority-building event
Your result should include

A practical next step, not just a label.
